## Fluval Plant 4.0 LED Review: The Upgrade the 3.0 Needed

If you've been eyeing the Fluval Plant 3.0, you should know it's been replaced. The Plant 4.0 is Fluval's updated flagship planted tank light, and after running the 59-89 cm model on my 200-litre planted community tank for several months, I can say the upgrade is worth it.

At $275-$300 AUD, it's not cheap. But if you're serious about growing plants, this light makes everything easier.

## The Specs

The 59-89 cm model packs 168 LEDs running at 32 watts. It's a full spectrum unit with independently controllable colour channels — you can fine-tune the balance between cool white, warm white, red, green, blue, and pink wavelengths. That level of control means you can optimise for plant growth during the day and switch to a moody blue moonlight in the evening.

The extendable brackets fit tanks from 59 to 89 cm, which covers most standard 3-foot setups. Build quality is solid — aluminium housing with a slim profile that looks clean on the tank.

## App Control Only — No Manual Buttons

This is the big change from the 3.0. The Plant 4.0 runs exclusively through the FluvalConnect app via Bluetooth. There are no physical buttons on the light itself. Once you pair it (which takes about 30 seconds), you set your schedule, intensity, and colour balance through the app.

The app works well once it's set up. I programmed a gradual sunrise over 30 minutes, peak intensity for 6 hours, then a 30-minute sunset into moonlight mode. Set it once and haven't touched it since.

The downside is obvious — if your phone dies or the app has issues, you can't manually override. In practice this hasn't been a problem for me, but it's worth knowing going in.

## Plant Growth

This is where the 4.0 earns its price. The PAR output at 30 cm depth is significantly higher than budget lights — enough to grow demanding species without CO2, and with CO2 you can push into high-light territory.

What's thrived under it:
- **Rotala rotundifolia** — deep pink tips, compact growth
- **Monte Carlo** — actually carpeting without CO2 (slowly, but it's happening)
- **Cryptocoryne wendtii** — lush and full
- **Ludwigia repens** — intense red colouration
- **Amazon sword** — massive growth, throwing runners

The colour rendering is gorgeous too. Fish look their best under this light — my cardinal tetras and cherry shrimp practically glow.

## What I Liked

- **Excellent PAR output** — grows everything from easy plants to demanding species.
- **Independently controllable channels** — dial in exactly the spectrum you want.
- **Sunrise/sunset scheduling** — gradual ramps reduce algae and look great.
- **Build quality** — aluminium housing, solid brackets, runs cool.
- **Aussie plug** — 240V Australian standard, no adapter needed.
- **Slim profile** — looks clean and modern on the tank.

## What I Didn't Like

- **Price** — $275-$300 AUD is a lot for a light. You're paying for the Fluval name and the app ecosystem.
- **App-only control** — no physical buttons means you're dependent on your phone and Bluetooth.
- **Bluetooth range** — works fine within a few metres but don't expect to control it from another room.
- **Overkill for low-tech** — if you're just growing java fern and anubias, this is way more light than you need. Save your money and grab a budget option instead.

## Is It Worth the Upgrade from the 3.0?

If you're already running a 3.0 and it's working fine, there's no urgent reason to upgrade. The 4.0 is brighter and the app is smoother, but the 3.0 still grows plants perfectly well.

If you're buying fresh though, go straight for the 4.0. It's the current model, it'll get app updates longer, and the LED array is a meaningful step up.

## Who Should Buy This?

The Fluval Plant 4.0 is the right light if you're running a mid-to-high tech planted tank and want something you can set up once and forget about. It's also a great choice if you care about colour rendering — your fish and plants will look their absolute best.

If you're on a budget or running a simple low-tech setup, you don't need this. A $25 PYPABL or similar budget light will do the job for a fraction of the price.

## Final Verdict — 4.6/5

The Fluval Plant 4.0 is the best planted tank light you can buy in Australia right now for tanks up to 90 cm. The full spectrum output, app scheduling, and build quality are all excellent. The price stings and the app-only control won't suit everyone, but if you want a premium light that delivers serious plant growth, this is it.
