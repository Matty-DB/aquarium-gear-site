## Hygger CO2 Regulator Review: Budget CO2 Done Right?

Setting up pressurised CO2 on a planted tank used to mean spending $200+ just on the regulator. The Hygger CO2 Regulator changes that — at under $100 AUD on Amazon AU, it's one of the most affordable complete regulators you can buy in Australia.

I've been running one on my 120-litre planted tank for about four months now. Here's whether the low price comes with compromises.

## What's in the Box

The Hygger comes as a mostly complete package: dual gauge regulator body, 12V DC solenoid valve, bubble counter with check valve, a syringe for filling the bubble counter, and the necessary fittings. The body is aluminium alloy — feels solid in the hand and shows no signs of corrosion after months of use.

It fits standard CGA320 CO2 cylinders, which is the most common thread you'll find at welding shops and homebrew stores across Australia. Screw it on, connect your tubing, and you're basically ready to go.

## Dual Gauge Display

Having two gauges is genuinely useful. The high-pressure gauge shows how much CO2 is left in your cylinder — so you know when a refill is coming instead of waking up to an empty tank. The low-pressure gauge shows your working pressure, which is what you actually adjust day-to-day.

The gauges are easy to read and have been accurate in my experience. I cross-checked against a mate's CO2Art regulator and the readings were within a couple of PSI, which is close enough for aquarium use.

## Solenoid Valve

The integrated 12V DC solenoid is the feature that makes this a proper planted tank regulator rather than just a pressure reducer. Plug it into a timer synced with your lights — CO2 kicks on an hour before lights, shuts off when lights go off. No wasted gas overnight, no pH crashes while fish are sleeping.

The solenoid runs quiet. There's a faint click when it opens and closes, but nothing you'd hear from across the room. After four months of daily cycling, it's still snapping open and shut reliably.

## Bubble Counter and Check Valve

The upgraded bubble counter is a nice touch. It comes with a syringe so you can top up the water without disconnecting anything — sounds minor but it's one of those quality-of-life details that shows Hygger thought about the user experience.

The built-in check valve prevents water backflow into the regulator. This is important — water getting into your regulator body is how cheap setups die. Having it integrated rather than needing to buy a separate inline check valve is a plus.

## Needle Valve Performance

This is where budget regulators usually fall down, and the Hygger is... adequate. The needle valve holds a steady bubble rate once you dial it in, but fine adjustments require patience. It's not as precise as what you'd get on a CO2Art or GLA regulator — sometimes a tiny turn jumps you from 1 bubble per second to 3. But once it's set, it stays put.

I'm running about 2 bubbles per second on my 120-litre tank and the drop checker sits at lime green by the time lights come on. Hasn't drifted in weeks.

## What I Liked

- **Price** — under $100 AUD delivered. Hard to beat for a complete regulator with solenoid.
- **Dual gauges** — know your cylinder level and working pressure at a glance.
- **Integrated solenoid** — proper timer control, not just manual on/off.
- **Bubble counter with syringe** — convenient top-up without disconnecting.
- **Build quality** — aluminium body feels solid, no corrosion after 4 months.
- **CGA320 fitting** — works with standard Aussie CO2 cylinders.

## What I Didn't Like

- **Needle valve precision** — functional but not as fine as premium regulators. Needs patience to dial in.
- **Single stage** — this is a single-stage regulator, which means end-of-tank dump is possible. Keep an eye on your cylinder pressure and swap before it gets very low.
- **Instructions** — the included guide is bare minimum. If this is your first CO2 setup, watch a YouTube tutorial alongside it.
- **No mounting bracket** — you'll need to figure out how to secure it to your stand or cabinet yourself.

## End-of-Tank Dump Warning

Because this is a single-stage regulator, as your CO2 cylinder empties the working pressure can spike — potentially dumping a dangerous amount of CO2 into your tank. This is a risk with any single-stage unit, not just the Hygger.

The fix is simple: watch your high-pressure gauge. When it drops below about 200 PSI, swap the cylinder. Don't try to squeeze every last bit out of it. If you want complete protection from end-of-tank dump, you need a dual-stage regulator, but those start at $200+ AUD.

## Who Should Buy This?

The Hygger is the right regulator if you're setting up pressurised CO2 for the first time and don't want to spend $200+ before you even know if you'll stick with it. It does everything a planted tank needs — solenoid control, bubble counting, pressure monitoring — at a price that makes CO2 injection accessible.

If you're already running CO2 and want something bulletproof with a finer needle valve and dual-stage protection, save up for a CO2Art Pro SE or GLA. But for most hobbyists growing mid-demand plants, the Hygger gets the job done.

## Final Verdict — 4.3/5

The Hygger CO2 Regulator is the best entry-level pressurised CO2 regulator available in Australia right now. Under $100 AUD with a solenoid, dual gauges, and a bubble counter — that's a lot of value. The needle valve could be more precise and the single-stage design means you need to watch your cylinder level, but for the price, it's a genuinely solid way to get into CO2 injection without breaking the bank.
