## About This Site

Aquarium Gear Guide publishes independent reviews and comparisons of aquarium equipment for freshwater and marine fishkeepers.

Our editorial focus is on buyer-intent content — helping aquarium enthusiasts choose the right filter, light, or CO2 system for their specific tank setup.

All equipment is evaluated based on published specifications, user reports, and aquarium hobby community experience. We cover freshwater planted tanks, cichlid setups, and reef aquariums.
